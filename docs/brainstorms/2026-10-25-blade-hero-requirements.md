# Second Playable Hero: Blade Fighter

## Summary

A second playable hero: a female blade fighter. Any player can join at any time and pick a hero, and the first player to confirm a hero gets it. She uses the brawler's controls. Behind them are a longer-reach, slower sword combo, a dash strike, and Guard in place of Grab.

---

## Problem Frame

The combat slice has one hero. In co-op, both players play the same character and are told apart only by accent colour, and there is nothing to choose at the title. The slice proved the pipeline can turn pinned kits into lit, normal-mapped characters and that the sim stays exact across hosts. It has not shown that a second character with a different feel fits the same controls, the same upgrade rule and the same gates.

The pinned kits have armed clips for her idle (Sword_Idle), three combo hits (Sword_Regular_A and B with recoveries, Sword_Regular_C), a dash (Sword_Dash), a heavy combo (Sword_Heavy_Combo) and a block (Sword_Block). They have no armed walk, run, hurt or knockdown clips, no sword mesh, and no female body in the pinned base-character files.

---

## Key Flows

- F1. Join and pick
  - **Trigger:** A pad that is not in the game presses Start, at the title or during a fight.
  - **Steps:** The player moves a cursor between the two heroes and confirms. Confirming locks that hero to the player. A hero locked by another player cannot be confirmed. At the title, a confirmed player's Start begins the fight. During a fight, the joiner spawns at the camera edge after confirming.
  - **Outcome:** Each player holds one hero, and no two players hold the same hero.
  - **Covered by:** R1, R2, R3, R4

---

## Requirements

**Selection**
- R1. Any player can join at any time, at the title or during a fight, and picks a hero before playing.
- R2. Confirming a hero locks it to that player. The first player to confirm a hero gets it, and another player cannot confirm a locked hero.
- R3. A solo player can play either hero.
- R4. A player whose pad disconnects keeps their locked hero. Reconnecting returns them to it. A player who disconnects at the title before confirming leaves the game.
- R5. A return to the title, after game over or clear, unlocks every hero.

**Blade fighter moveset**
- R6. Attack is a 3-hit sword combo with visibly longer reach than the brawler's combo and a longer total duration. It chains and resets on the brawler's rule.
- R7. Dash is a sword dash strike that hits enemies along its path.
- R8. The Grab button holds Guard; she has no grab or throw. While guarding she cannot move, attack, jump or dash, and move input turns her in place. Releasing the button returns her to idle.
- R9. A hit from the front while guarding deals no damage, pushes her back with a short flinch, does not turn her, and grants no invulnerability. A hit from behind lands as normal and turns her to face the attacker, so her front is then measured from the new facing.
- R10. The upgrade after wave 2 offers her the knockback special and the dash special with the same game effect as the brawler's, drawn with sword clips.
- R11. Everything else follows the brawler's rules: move, jump, the jump attack (shown with the brawler's placeholder jump frame), hurt, knockdown, get-up, lives, and the upgrade pick.

**Look**
- R12. She uses a female body from a pinned kit: the Universal Base Characters female body, or UAL2's Mannequin_F if that pack has none. She has a neon retexture and a silhouette that reads as a different character from the brawler at 128 px.
- R13. Her sword is generated by the render script (handle, guard, blade) and stays in her hand in every clip, including walk, hurt and knockdown.
- R14. Her blade glows: an additive glow layer drawn over the blade in every frame, in her player's accent colour.
- R15. Each player keeps their HUD accent colour (P1 magenta, P2 cyan) on whichever hero they play, so both heroes exist in both accents.
- R16. The HUD names each player's hero.

**Evidence**
- R17. The brawler-gate scripts include a two-player run with the brawler and the blade fighter together, and pass on every host with exact state.
- R18. The look set adds the blade fighter under one reference light, mid-combo beside a grunt, and guarding.

---

## Acceptance Examples

- AE1. **Covers R1, R2.** Given two pads at the title choosing at the same time, when P2 confirms the blade fighter first, P2 holds her and P1 can confirm only the brawler.
- AE2. **Covers R1, R2.** Given a solo brawler mid-fight, when a second pad presses Start, it picks from the free heroes, can confirm only the blade fighter, and spawns at the camera edge.
- AE3. **Covers R8, R9.** Given the blade fighter guarding and facing a grunt, when the grunt's punch lands, she loses no health, slides back briefly and keeps facing it. When a second grunt hits her from behind inside that flinch, the hit lands and she turns to face the second grunt.
- AE4. **Covers R9.** Given the blade fighter guarding, when the heavy's hook lands from the front, she loses no health and is pushed back further than by a grunt.
- AE5. **Covers R4.** Given P2 holding the blade fighter mid-fight, when P2's pad disconnects and a third pad presses Start, the third pad cannot confirm the blade fighter. When P2 reconnects, P2 plays her again.

---

## Success Criteria

- Marcus plays co-op natively with one controller per player, picks heroes from the title and mid-fight, and the two heroes play differently on the same controls: longer reach, slower combo, and Guard.
- The brawler-only runs stay byte-identical to the current goldens, and the brawler gate passes with a run that has both heroes.
- The look set gains the blade fighter captures in both accents.

---

## Scope Boundaries

- No blade-specific upgrade pair.
- No grab or throw for her.
- No hand-keyed clips for her; only kit clips and frame ranges.
- No duplicate heroes.
- No new enemies, arenas or waves.
- No enemy behaviour against Guard; Guard does not break.

---

## Key Decisions

- First to confirm gets the hero: anyone can join and pick at any time, with no turn order.
- Guard replaces Grab: the one new system, using the kit's Sword_Block.
- Same upgrade variants for both heroes: the upgrade screen and its rule are unchanged.
- Per-player accents on both heroes: the look wins. The 64 MiB atlas budget in ADR-0002 was our own number and is retired; atlas memory is measured against the cart's real wasm memory limit instead.
- Real blade glow now through an additive overlay, the same technique as the knockback ring.
- Generated sword, kept in hand in every clip.
- Starting values for Guard: unbreakable, longer pushback from the heavy. All are tuned in play.

---

## Dependencies / Assumptions

- Marcus downloads the free Universal Base Characters pack by hand so the female body can be pinned, as was done for UAL2. Its licence is checked by the kit pin, like the other kits.
- The goldens stay unchanged for brawler-only runs: the hero field enters the checksum only when the title flow is present. Runs that go through the new title flow get new goldens.
- Beyond the goldens, these gates change: the title checks in ui-check and the title-contrast check, the look-set screen assertions, the bot scripts (the bot learns her moves and Guard), the gate-strict whitelist (new hero fields), and reach-check (her widest frame may change the camera allowance).
